# Video narration requirements

Every video must speak clean prose only. Never narrate metadata labels such as Title, Topic, Narrative, Primary source URL, or URL; never read URLs, code fences, or production directions. Keep source links and packaging in their separate fields.

Clean pasted narratives before sentence splitting, preserve medical numbers and meaningful prose, and validate the exact text sent to TTS. Changes must retain regression coverage for whole Claude responses and URLs split by whitespace. A prompt reminder alone is not a fix.

Keep narration-only audio and localized engagement closings. Before replacing a faulty upload, make the original private, render the replacement privately, inspect its narration audit and rendered audio, then release it and verify YouTube and the saved broadcast record. Do not announce a private review upload on social media.
